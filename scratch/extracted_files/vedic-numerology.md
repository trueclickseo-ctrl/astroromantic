**Vedic numerology**, known in Sanskrit as **Anka Shastra**, is the Indian system of reading personality, destiny, and life timing through numbers drawn from your birth date and name. Each number from 1 to 9 is linked to one of the nine Navagraha planets, so every numerology reading is, at its core, a planetary reading expressed through numbers. The three foundational numbers — Moolank, Bhagyank, and Namank — together map your instinctive nature, your life direction, and the vibration carried by your name.

## Moolank, Bhagyank, Namank: The Three Core Numbers

| Number | Sanskrit Term | Calculated From | Reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Psychic Number | Moolank | Day of birth only | Instinctive temperament, self-image |
| Destiny Number | Bhagyank | Full date of birth (day+month+year) | Life direction, opportunities, long-term path |
| Name Number | Namank | Full name letters | Vibration your name carries in the world |

## How to Calculate Your Moolank

Your Moolank comes from your **day of birth alone** — month and year don't factor in.

1. Take the day of the month you were born.
2. If it's already a single digit (1–9), that's your Moolank.
3. If it's two digits (10–31), add the digits together until you reach a single number.

**Example:** Born on the 23rd → 2 + 3 = **5**. Moolank = 5.

Moolank is read as your first, instinctive layer of temperament — how you act and react before conscious thought or life experience refines your behavior.

## How to Calculate Your Bhagyank

Your Bhagyank uses your **entire date of birth** — day, month, and year — added together and reduced to a single digit.

**Example:** Born April 5, 1974
- Day: 5
- Month: 4
- Year: 1+9+7+4 = 21 → 2+1 = 3
- Total: 5 + 4 + 3 = 12 → 1+2 = **3**

Bhagyank = 3

Where Moolank shows how you naturally act, Bhagyank shows where your life is generally headed — many numerologists say its influence becomes especially prominent from your mid-30s onward.

## How to Calculate Your Namank

Namank converts every letter in your full name into a number using a standard Vedic letter-to-number chart, adds the totals, and reduces to a single digit — similar in method to Western name numerology, but using planetary associations specific to the Vedic system.

## Vedic Numerology vs. Western Numerology

| | Vedic Numerology | Western (Pythagorean) Numerology |
|---|---|---|
| **Planetary association** | Rahu = 4, Ketu = 7 (shadow planets) | Uranus, Neptune generally not tied to core numbers |
| **Core numbers** | Moolank, Bhagyank, Namank | Life Path, Destiny/Expression, Soul Urge |
| **Name letter 9** | No letter carries value 9 in some Vedic charts | 9 assigned normally (I, R) |
| **Cultural roots** | Rooted in the Vedas and Jyotish | Rooted in Pythagorean Greek tradition |
| **Best used with** | Full Vedic Kundli for cross-verification | Standalone or alongside Western astrology |

## Why Vedic Numerology Complements a Full Kundli

Because Moolank and Bhagyank need only a birth date — no birth time — numerology is often used as a **quick, accessible entry point** into Vedic self-understanding, especially when someone's exact birth time isn't known and a full Lagna-based Kundli isn't yet possible. That said, most practitioners treat numerology as a complement to, not a substitute for, a complete birth chart reading, since it can't capture house placements, Dashas, or divisional charts.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Confusing Moolank with Bhagyank** — Moolank uses only the day of birth; Bhagyank uses the entire date.
- **Mixing Vedic and Western letter-to-number charts** for Namank calculations, which produces inconsistent results.
- **Treating numerology as a full substitute** for a complete Kundli, which also requires birth time and location.
- **Ignoring the planetary layer** — each number's meaning is rooted in its ruling Navagraha planet, not just an abstract digit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the difference between Moolank and Bhagyank?
Moolank comes only from your day of birth and reflects instinctive personality, while Bhagyank comes from your complete date of birth and reflects your broader destiny and life direction.

### Do I need my birth time for Vedic numerology?
No — Moolank, Bhagyank, and Namank all use only your date of birth and full name, unlike a complete Kundli, which requires exact birth time and location for house placements.

### Why does Vedic numerology use Rahu and Ketu instead of Uranus and Neptune?
Vedic numerology is built on the Navagraha (nine Vedic planets), which include the shadow planets Rahu and Ketu rather than the outer planets used in Western astronomy-based systems.

### Can Moolank and Bhagyank conflict with each other?
Yes — when your instinctive Moolank nature and your broader Bhagyank direction pull differently, numerologists describe this as a common source of internal tension worth understanding rather than ignoring.

### Is Vedic numerology the same as Chaldean numerology?
No — Chaldean numerology is a separate, older letter-based system focused on name calculations using 1–8 values, while Vedic numerology (Anka Shastra) is rooted specifically in the Navagraha planetary framework and birth-date numbers.

### How accurate is Vedic numerology compared to a full birth chart?
Numerology offers a faster, date-and-name-based lens into personality and direction, but a complete Kundli — with birth time, houses, and Dashas — provides significantly more detailed and individualized insight.
