## Vedic Astrology Guides

Vedic astrology (Jyotish) reads your birth chart through the sidereal zodiac, centers on your Moon sign rather than your Sun sign, and uses a distinct set of tools — Nakshatras, Dashas, and the Navagraha planets — not found in Western practice.

- **[Zodiac Signs (Rashi)](/vedic/zodiac-signs)** — the 12 Vedic signs, how they differ from Western signs, and why your Rashi might surprise you
- **[Planets (Navagraha)](/vedic/planets)** — the nine planetary forces, including the shadow planets Rahu and Ketu
- **[Houses (Bhava)](/vedic/houses)** — the 12 life-area divisions of a Vedic chart, from self to spiritual liberation
- **[Numerology (Anka Shastra)](/vedic/numerology)** — Moolank, Bhagyank, and Namank, calculated from your birth date and name
- **[KP Astrology](/vedic/kp-astrology)** — Krishnamurti Paddhati's precise, sub-lord-based system for timing life events

## Western Astrology Guides

Western astrology reads your birth chart through the tropical zodiac, anchored to the seasons rather than the physical constellations, and centers on your Sun sign, Moon sign, and Rising sign together.

- **[Overview](/western/overview)** — a complete beginner's map of how a Western birth chart comes together
- **[Zodiac Signs](/western/zodiac-signs)** — all 12 sun signs, their dates, elements, and ruling planets
- **[Planets](/western/planets)** — the 10 planets from the Sun to Pluto, and what each one governs
- **[Houses](/western/houses)** — the 12 life-area divisions, including the Ascendant and Midheaven
- **[Aspects](/western/aspects)** — how planets interact through conjunctions, trines, squares, and oppositions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I start with Vedic or Western astrology?
Neither is more "correct" — they're two distinct traditions with different zodiacs and methods; many people are naturally drawn to whichever framework their culture or a specific report has already introduced them to.

### Do I need to read every guide in order?
No — each guide is written to stand on its own, though the Vedic and Western sections build most naturally from Zodiac Signs through Planets, Houses, and finally the more specialized topics.

### What's the difference between the "Vedic Astrology" section and "Report Guides"?
The Vedic and Western sections explain the underlying concepts and systems, while Report Guides explain the specific, personalized results generated from your own birth details.

### I don't know my exact birth time — which guides can I still use?
Sun sign and Moolank/Bhagyank-based content (Western Zodiac Signs, Vedic Numerology) only need your birth date, while anything involving houses, Ascendant, or Dasha timing requires an exact birth time for accuracy.

### Where should I start if I just received my Kundli report?
Start with Life Insights, since it's built as the foundational overview every other specialized report (Dasha, doshas, yogas) assumes you've already read.
