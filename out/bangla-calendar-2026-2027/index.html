Bangladesh Bangla Civil Months (বাংলা ১২ মাস)

Under the Bangladesh Bangla Academy rules, month lengths are standardized into fixed 31-day and 30-day periods.

Month 1

Boishakh (বৈশাখ)

Grisma
Gregorian: Apr 14 – May 14 (31 Days)

Month 1 of Bangladesh Civil Calendar. Pohela Boishakh ALWAYS falls on April 14 in Bangladesh.

Month 2

Joishtho (জ্যৈষ্ঠ)

Grisma
Gregorian: May 15 – Jun 14 (31 Days)

Month 2. Fixed 31 days. Season of summer fruits (Juicy mangoes, jackfruits, lychees).

Month 3

Asharh (আষাঢ়)

Barsa
Gregorian: Jun 15 – Jul 15 (31 Days)

Month 3. Fixed 31 days. Arrival of monsoon rains across Bangladesh river basins.

Month 4

Shrabon (শ্রাবণ)

Barsa
Gregorian: Jul 16 – Aug 15 (31 Days)

Month 4. Fixed 31 days. Heavy rain showers, lush paddy fields, riverine fish season.

Month 5

Bhadro (ভাদ্র)

Sarat
Gregorian: Aug 16 – Sep 16 (31 Days)

Month 5. Fixed 31 days. Transition from monsoon to early autumn.

Month 6

Ashwin (আশ্বিন)

Sarat
Gregorian: Sep 17 – Oct 16 (30 Days)

Month 6. Fixed 30 days. Autumn breezes, Durga Puja, white cloud skies.

Month 7

Kartik (কার্তিক)

Hemanta
Gregorian: Oct 17 – Nov 15 (30 Days)

Month 7. Fixed 30 days. Dewy mornings, autumn harvest preparations.

Month 8

Agrahayan (অগ্রহায়ণ)

Hemanta
Gregorian: Nov 16 – Dec 15 (30 Days)

Month 8. Fixed 30 days. Nabanna festival, harvesting Aman rice.

Month 9

Poush (পৌষ)

Sit
Gregorian: Dec 16 – Jan 14 (30 Days)

Month 9. Fixed 30 days. Cool winter weather, Pouthe-puli, date-palm syrup.

Month 10

Magh (মাঘ)

Sit
Gregorian: Jan 15 – Feb 13 (30 Days)

Month 10. Fixed 30 days. Winter chill, mustard flower fields.

Month 11

Falgun (ফাল্গুন)

Basanta
Gregorian: Feb 14 – Mar 14 (30 Days)

Month 11. Fixed 30 days. Pohela Falgun (Spring Day), Ekushey February.

Month 12

Chaitra (চৈত্র)

Basanta
Gregorian: Mar 15 – Apr 13 (30/31 Days)

Month 12. 30 days (31 in leap years). Year-end month before Pohela Boishakh.

What Is the Bangladesh Bangla Calendar?

The Bangla Calendar used in Bangladesh is the country's official civil national calendar. In 1966, a committee formed under the Bangla Academy and headed by eminent scholar Dr. Muhammad Shahidullah modified the traditional calendar rules to align with civil governance.

Key rules of the Bangladesh Civil Bangla Calendar:

  • Fixed Pohela Boishakh: New Year Day (Pohela Boishakh) ALWAYS falls on April 14 in Gregorian calendar.
  • First 5 Months: Boishakh, Joishtho, Asharh, Shrabon, and Bhadro are fixed at 31 days each.
  • Remaining 7 Months: Ashwin, Kartik, Agrahayan, Poush, Magh, Falgun, and Chaitra are fixed at 30 days each (Chaitra has 31 days during Gregorian leap years).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the Bangladesh Bangla Calendar 2026–2027?▼

The Bangladesh Bangla calendar 2026–2027 is the official civil national calendar of Bangladesh. Revised by the Bangla Academy committee (headed by Dr. Muhammad Shahidullah), it standardizes month lengths and fixes Pohela Boishakh on April 14 every year.

What is the Bangla year in 2026?▼

The year 2026 corresponds to Bangla Year 1432 (until April 13, 2026) and Bangla Year 1433 (which begins on Pohela Boishakh / April 14, 2026).

When is Pohela Boishakh in Bangladesh?▼

In Bangladesh, Pohela Boishakh (Bangla New Year) is ALWAYS celebrated on April 14 as per the official government civil calendar.

What are the month lengths in the Bangladesh civil Bangla calendar?▼

The first 5 months (Boishakh, Joishtho, Asharh, Shrabon, Bhadro) have 31 days each. The remaining 7 months (Ashwin, Kartik, Agrahayan, Poush, Magh, Falgun, Chaitra) have 30 days each (Chaitra has 31 days in leap years).

How does the Bangladesh Bangla calendar differ from the Indian Bengali Panjika?▼

Bangladesh uses a fixed civil calendar where Pohela Boishakh is always April 14 and month days are set (31 or 30 days). The Indian Bengali Panjika is an astronomical lunisolar almanac where month lengths vary based on exact solar transits (Sankranti) and Poila Boishakh usually falls on April 15.

What are the major national public holidays in Bangladesh?▼

Major national public holidays in Bangladesh include Language Martyrs' Day / International Mother Language Day (Ekushey Feb 21), Independence & National Day (March 26), Pohela Boishakh (April 14), National Mourning Day (August 15), and Victory Day (December 16).
