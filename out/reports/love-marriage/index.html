Love & Marriage Report: Understanding Your Compatibility Reading

Your **Love & Marriage report** is built on **Guna Milan**, also called **Ashtakoot Milan** — the traditional Vedic astrology method for evaluating compatibility between two people before marriage. It compares eight distinct compatibility factors (kootas) between partners' birth charts, producing a score out of 36 points, then layers in additional dosha checks like Mangal Dosha for a fuller picture of marital harmony.

Key Takeaways

  • •A **Love & Marriage report** evaluates romantic and marital compatibility using **Guna Milan (Ashtakoot matching)** — an eight-factor, 36-point Vedic compatibility system.
  • •The score is calculated almost entirely from each partner's **Moon sign and Nakshatra (birth star)**, not their Sun sign or Lagna.
  • •A score of **18 or above out of 36** is traditionally considered acceptable for marriage, though the specific kootas that scored low matter as much as the total.
  • •**Nadi Dosha** (0 points in the Nadi koota) is considered the most serious individual defect, even outweighing an otherwise high total score.
  • •The report also checks **Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha)** separately, since Mars placement issues aren't captured within the standard Ashtakoot score.

What Is Guna Milan (Ashtakoot Matching)?

"Ashtakoot" breaks down into *ashta* (eight) and *koota* (category), reflecting the eight distinct dimensions of compatibility this system evaluates. Unlike a general love calculator based on names, Guna Milan is calculated from each partner's **Moon sign (Rashi) and Nakshatra (birth star)** — because in Vedic astrology, the Moon governs the mind and emotions, making it the most relevant placement for assessing how two people will actually get along day to day.

The 8 Kootas and Their Points

KootaMax PointsWhat It Measures
Varna1Spiritual compatibility, working temperament
Vashya2Mutual attraction and control dynamic
Tara3General wellbeing and destiny alignment
Yoni4Physical and sexual compatibility
Graha Maitri5Mental compatibility and friendship
Gana6Temperament and behavioral compatibility

Nadi8Health, genetic compatibility, progeny
**Total****36**

Notice that the points increase with importance — Nadi carries the most weight (8 points) because it's considered the most consequential factor for long-term health and family compatibility, while Varna carries the least (1 point).

What Your Total Score Means

Score RangeGeneral Interpretation

Below 18Generally considered insufficient without further review

A score of **18 or above** is the traditional threshold, though most astrologers stress that the total number alone doesn't tell the whole story — which specific kootas scored low matters just as much as the total.

Why the Total Score Isn't the Whole Picture

A combined score of 20 with a full 8-point Nadi match can indicate stronger real-world compatibility than a 24 that includes **Nadi Dosha** (0 points in the Nadi koota) — because Nadi Dosha is considered the most serious individual defect in the entire system, traditionally linked to health and progeny concerns regardless of how well the other seven kootas score.

Other Doshas Checked Alongside Guna Milan

Beyond the 36-point Ashtakoot score, a complete Love & Marriage report typically checks for these additional compatibility concerns:

  • **Nadi Dosha** — same Nadi between partners; considered the most serious defect
  • **Bhakoot Dosha** — adverse Moon-sign combinations (6/8 or 2/12 positions), affecting emotional and financial harmony
  • **Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha)** — Mars placed in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house of either partner's individual chart, checked separately from the Ashtakoot score

How to Read Your Love & Marriage Report

  1. **Start with the total Guna score** as a general baseline, not a final verdict.
  1. **Check which specific kootas scored low** — a low Nadi or Bhakoot score deserves more attention than a low Varna score.
  1. **Review the Mangal Dosha section separately** — this isn't part of the 36-point total but significantly affects marital compatibility if present in one or both charts.
  1. **Look at any cancellation conditions** — certain planetary combinations can cancel or reduce the effect of a dosha even when it's technically present.
  1. **Remember the human factor** — mutual understanding, communication, and shared values remain the foundation of a successful relationship regardless of the numerical score.

Common Mistakes When Reading a Love & Marriage Report

  • **Fixating on the total score alone** without checking which individual kootas are weak.
  • **Treating a below-18 score as an automatic red flag** rather than one input among many relationship factors.
  • **Ignoring Mangal Dosha** because it isn't part of the 36-point Ashtakoot total.
  • **Assuming a high score guarantees a happy marriage** — Guna Milan measures astrological alignment, not day-to-day compatibility factors like communication style or shared goals.

What is a good Guna Milan score for marriage?

A score of 18 or above out of 36 is traditionally considered acceptable, with scores above 24 generally seen as very good, though the specific kootas involved matter as much as the total.

Is Guna Milan based on names or birth details?

It's based on birth details specifically — each partner's Moon sign and Nakshatra — not names, which distinguishes it from a general name-based love calculator.

What is Nadi Dosha and why is it considered so serious?

Nadi Dosha occurs when both partners share the same Nadi, scoring 0 out of 8 in that koota; it's traditionally linked to health and progeny concerns and is considered the most consequential defect in the entire matching system.

Is Mangal Dosha part of the 36-point Guna Milan score?

No — Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha) is checked as a separate factor based on Mars's placement in each partner's individual chart, alongside but distinct from the Ashtakoot score.

Can a low Guna Milan score be overridden?

Yes — astrologers look for specific planetary cancellation conditions that can reduce or neutralize an otherwise concerning dosha, and many also weigh the couple's broader charts, not the score in isolation.

Does a high Guna Milan score guarantee a successful marriage?

No — Guna Milan measures traditional astrological alignment, but real-world relationship success also depends on communication, values, and effort that a compatibility score can't measure.
