All Astrology Guides: Vedic, Western & Report Guides

Welcome to the complete library of astrology guides on AstroRomantic. Whether you're brand new to astrology or trying to make sense of a specific report you just generated, this page organizes everything in one place — grouped the same way you'll find it in the navigation menu: Vedic Astrology, Western Astrology, and Report Guides.

Key Takeaways

  • •This hub organizes all astrology content into three sections: Vedic Astrology, Western Astrology, and Report Guides — matching how the site's navigation is structured.
  • •Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac and centers on your Moon sign (Rashi), while Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac and centers on your Sun sign — start with whichever tradition matches what you're most curious about.
  • •Report Guides explain the specific, personalized reports generated from your birth chart — start with Life Insights, since it's the foundational full-chart reading every other report builds on.
  • •New to astrology entirely? Start with Western Overview or Vedic Zodiac Signs — both are written for complete beginners.
  • •Already have a Kundli or birth chart? Jump straight to the Report Guides section to understand what your results mean.

Where to Start

If you're not sure where to begin, here's a simple way to think about it:

  • Curious about your Kundli, Rashi, or a Vedic-specific term? Start in the Vedic Astrology section.
  • More familiar with sun signs, horoscope columns, and the "Big Three"? Start in the Western Astrology section.
  • Already generated a report and want to understand what it means? Go straight to Report Guides.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I start with Vedic or Western astrology?▼

Neither is more "correct" — they're two distinct traditions with different zodiacs and methods; many people are naturally drawn to whichever framework their culture or a specific report has already introduced them to.

Do I need to read every guide in order?▼

No — each guide is written to stand on its own, though the Vedic and Western sections build most naturally from Zodiac Signs through Planets, Houses, and finally the more specialized topics.

What's the difference between the "Vedic Astrology" section and "Report Guides"?▼

The Vedic and Western sections explain the underlying concepts and systems, while Report Guides explain the specific, personalized results generated from your own birth details.

I don't know my exact birth time — which guides can I still use?▼

Sun sign and Moolank/Bhagyank-based content (Western Zodiac Signs, Vedic Numerology) only need your birth date, while anything involving houses, Ascendant, or Dasha timing requires an exact birth time for accuracy.

Where should I start if I just received my Kundli report?▼

Start with Life Insights, since it's built as the foundational overview every other specialized report (Dasha, doshas, yogas) assumes you've already read.
